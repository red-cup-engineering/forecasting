# Kalshi microstructure and a queue-aware fill model, 2026-10-04

Read-only study (public market data plus the desk's signed GETs through `kalshi-services-section/src/client.mjs`; no orders, cancels or policy edits).
Why: the live desk's resting orders almost never fill (weather sells: 4 filled orders, 12 of 401 contracts, in 210 orders / 80 order-hours; gas bids: 0 of 33). The 2026-10-03 backtest counted a fill whenever any trade printed at or through the price within 6h, ignoring who traded and the queue.

Layout: `scripts/` (all re-runnable: `scripts/analyze.sh [--grids]`), `results/` (tables), `data/` (raw tape and books, git-ignored, regenerable, about 100 MB).
Collector: `node scripts/collect.mjs --every 300 --until 2026-10-05T04:00:00Z` is resumable (state in `data/collect-state.json`); it samples ~360-400 candidate books every 5 min, the taker tape, hourly LIP windows, and the desk's `queue_position` reads. Stop it with `kill <pid of node scripts/collect.mjs>`. Re-run `scripts/analyze.sh` after it has collected more (the book-based tables in `books.md`, `gas.md`, `weather-ev.md` rest on only ~10 sweeps, 03:03-03:53Z, so far).
Method note: fill probabilities replay the public tape of 2,005 settled weather and ~2,200 gas markets (8.6 days) on a 10-minute placement grid. Queue ahead is a parameter (the tape has no book); live books supply which values occur.

## Findings

1. **Who takes.** Taker-buy-YES at 2-6c (what fills the desk's sells): median 7 contracts, 44% are 1-5 contracts, but 18% of same-second clusters walk 2+ price levels (one aggressive order) and carry 42% of contracts. At 1c, 82% of volume is in orders over 200 contracts (lottery bulk). Timing: 58% of contracts lift between 12:00 and 18:59 ET, 7% between 00:00 and 05:59 ET. By hours to close the volume per market is highest 6-24h out (~700 contracts per market), 257 inside 6h, 80 at 36-48h. 34 of 1,221 markets with such buys settled YES (3.3% of contracts). (`results/flow.md`)
2. **Queue.** Median contracts at the touch ask when the touch is 2c/3c/4c/5c/6c: 103/70/34/50/12. The signed `queue_position` equals book depth at the level minus the order's own size (checked on 10 desk orders), so the book predicts our queue exactly. The touch price changes in 8.5% of 5-minute pairs, its size in 21%, unchanged 71%. 49% of 2c-touch snapshots have no bid at all; a 1c improvement is possible (spread >= 2c) in 44% of 3c-touch snapshots. (`results/books.md`)
3. **Fill model: arrival rate, not queue, dominates.** Most "any" fills in the replay are *sweeps* (a buyer walks up through the level, clearing everyone), which are independent of queue. Queue-controlled ("clean") fills fall fast with Q: p=2c, 60 min: 8.2% at Q=0, 4.3% at Q=20, 1.4% at Q=100, 0.2% at Q=500. What moves the fill odds most is the market's trailing-6h taker-buy volume at the price: P(fill within 60 min, Q=0) is 8.6% when under 1 contract traded, 22% at 1-10, 34% at 50-200, 48% above 200 (`results/fill-model.md`). Certified far tails live in the quiet bucket. Higher price fills more: quiet, join at median queue, 4h: 2c 13.6%, 3c 11.7%, 4c 21.7%, 5c 25.2%, 6c 33.7%.
4. **Validation on the desk.** Replaying its 210 weather sells against the tape: only 8 (3.8%) ever saw any taker volume or sweep at their price, even at Q=0; 4 filled (the T102 2c order twice and B101.5 3c, both when first in queue). The pooled grid over-predicts the desk about 2.5x (20 fills at Q=0 vs ceiling 8) because the desk's markets are quiet; matched on the quiet bucket (trailing volume < 1) it predicts 5.0 vs ceiling 2 vs realised 2. Orders live a median 28 min (10th percentile 9.5 min): repricing and the 40 min TTL keep resetting queue position. Gas: 33 one-lot bids at 98/95c sat behind 1,000-3,400 contracts and saw no taker event at their price. (`results/desk-validation.md`)
5. **Adverse selection by channel.** Sweep-only fills of simulated weather sells earn about 0 to -0.6c per contract held (YES rate 2.0-6.1%); clean fills earn +0.7 to +4.3c (YES rate 0.7-2.8%). For gas favourite bids, sweep fills lose 3-9c a contract (the favourite is falling through your bid); clean fills are about break-even to +4c. Fills you get by *not* being first are the toxic ones.
6. **Incentive farmers.** 8,619 active LIP programs; **none on KXHIGH/KXLOWT**, all 510 daily gas and diesel ladders for 26OCT04 have one (target size 1,000, discount 50%, pool 1,000,000 or 200,000 units, window = the market's whole life, 12:00Z the day before to close). Consequence: gas touch bids hold >= 1,000 in 74-84% of 94-98c snapshots (median 1,533 at 98c), 59% of favourite books have no YES ask at all, and static 1,000+ levels also sit at 1c bids and 2c asks. A 1-lot's LIP share is ~1/5,000 of a pool: ignore it. Weather queues (100 at 2c) are ordinary competing sellers, not farmers; 7 static ~300-contract asks at 2c exist. (`results/books.md`, `books-static-*.csv`)
7. **Gas fill odds.** Joining a gas touch bid at the live queue: any-fill 11% per hour but only 1.4% clean (2.8% in 4h); where the queue is <= 100 (11-17% of snapshots, e.g. 95c touches) 14.7% any. At Q=1000: clean 0.3-0.8% per 20-60 min at 98c. (`results/gas.md`)

## Corrected weather-lane EV (cents per contract per resting placement; q = 1% true YES probability, makers pay 0)

| price | 30 min | 60 min | 4 h | (quiet market, join at median queue) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2c | 0.03 | 0.05 | 0.14 | |
| 3c | 0.05 | 0.09 | 0.23 | |
| 4c | 0.15 | 0.27 | 0.65 | |
| 5c | 0.25 | 0.44 | 1.01 | |
| 6c | 0.44 | 0.76 | 1.68 | |

At the desk's price mix (48% 2c, 31% 3c, 13% 4c, 5% 5c, 2.5% 6c) that is ~0.07c per contract per 30 min and ~0.32c per 4 h. The 2026-10-03 backtest implied about 1.6c (60% fill x +2.6c) for highs. Same placements, old rule vs queue-aware (4h horizon vs the old 6h): any-fill 52% -> 39% at Q=0 and 32% at median Q; quiet markets 29% -> 16%; clean fills only 3.5%. At ~25 distinct contracts of exposure a day this lane is worth roughly 5-15c a day, not 40c; capacity, not queue, is the next limit. Improving 1c inside barely moves fill odds (quiet, 60 min: 11.3% vs 9.1% when spread allows) and costs 1c of premium, so it loses EV at 2-4c; join the touch. Taking the bid (`results/taker-edge.md`): a bid >= 2c exists in 35% of tail snapshots, >= 3c in 22%; EV after the per-order cent-rounded fee is 0 for a 1-lot at 2c, +0.8c/contract at 5+ lots at 2c, +1.0 to +1.8c at 3c, so a 5+ contract batch at a >= 3c bid beats resting by 5-30x.

## Recommended execution policy

1. Weather: take (IOC into the bid) in batches of >= 5 contracts whenever the bid is >= 3c (>= 2c from 5 lots); rest only the remainder. Do not rest 2c and 3c asks in quiet markets (EV 0.03-0.23c); prefer >= 4c tails. Do not improve by 1c.
2. Stop churning: reprice/re-post only when the target moves >= 2c or certification lapses; use a TTL of ~2h (refresh only inside the last 30 min) so queue time accrues (fill probability is roughly linear in time at these rates: 30 min -> 4 h is ~4-5x). Cancel through the 10-minute cycle when uncertified; TTL is only the dead-desk safety net. Check whether a venue amend/decrease preserves priority.
3. Gas favourites: keep the lane paused for resting; disable resting bids on LIP series. Rest only where touch queue <= 100 and treat sweep fills as toxic; otherwise take an ask where one exists (41% of books) in batches >= 7.
4. Backtests: use `scripts/queue_aware_fill.py` (fill iff cumulative taker volume at the price exceeds Q0, or a taker crossed the level; Q0 = measured median by price, 0 when inside the spread) and report clean fills separately; condition on trailing taker volume, not the market average.

## Caveats
Ten sweeps (50 min) of books so far; time-of-day and LIP-window dynamics on the book side are pending the collector. Replay ignores cancels ahead of us (fills are slightly understated) and assumes instant visibility. Desk sample is clustered in ~10 markets. Yes-rates by channel are for uncertified brackets; certified tails should be lower. LIP pool units unverified. The `data/` tree and the whole record directory were swept once by another session's untracked-file stash (restored); keep `data/` ignored and copy the scripts if a stash recipe runs again.
