# Settleable in advance, 2026-10-18

Which Kalshi weather markets have an outcome the public observations have already determined before the market closes, how likely acting on that
is to be wrong, and what is on offer. The measurement and the strategy live in kalshi-services-section (`scripts/backtest-settleable-in-advance.mjs`,
`scripts/backtest-daily-determination.mjs`, `scripts/analyze-books.mjs`, `content/reports/2026-10-18-settleable-in-advance.md`); this record holds what
forecasting owns: the models, and the scripts that fetch the data they are derived from.

## Models (forecasting 0.6.10)

- `src/weather-index-move-tail.mjs`: how far Kalshi's city temperature index moves in `h` minutes, as a probability with a confidence bound (an empirical
  survival function below a threshold, a generalized Pareto tail above it, a moving-block bootstrap for the bound; volatility regimes; every parameter with its
  provenance in `PARAMETERS`). Replaces the rate envelope's "largest move seen times 1.5". Table: `content/config/weather-index-move-tail.json`
  (`scripts/hourly-index-move-tail.mjs`).
- `src/weather-extreme-discrepancy.mjs`, `src/weather-daily-observations.mjs`: how far a climate report's daily high, low or precipitation can sit from the
  station's observations (Clopper-Pearson bounds on 3,913 station-days of 2026), for the daily contracts whose extreme or total only moves one way. Table:
  `content/config/weather-extreme-discrepancy.json` (`scripts/daily-extreme-discrepancy.mjs`).

## Data (git-ignored, regenerated by the scripts here and in 2026-10-16-hourly-index-station-lattice)

`scripts/fetch-daily-families.mjs` (events, trades, one-minute candles of the daily series; IEM one-minute ASOS), `scripts/fetch-asos-history.mjs` (the
NWS Daily Climate Report values from IEM's parsed archive and the one-minute ASOS by month), `scripts/fetch-metar-history.mjs` (routine METARs with the
precipitation group), `scripts/sample-books.mjs` (the public order books of the open weather markets every five minutes: the venue keeps no history of its
books, so depth can only be measured forward).

## A finding in the house's own configuration

`content/config/rain-markets.json` had Houston on KIAH (CLIIAH); Kalshi's KXRAIN Houston market settles on CLIHOU (Hobby). KXRAIN-26OCT14-HOU resolved no
while the IAH gauge showed 0.05 inch or more; a desk that read the IAH gauge would have bought YES at 1 to 23 cents on 14,485 contracts, all lost. Houston is
corrected and set `validated: false` (its fit predates the correction); Detroit, Indianapolis, St. Louis and Tampa, which have KXRAIN markets, are added.
