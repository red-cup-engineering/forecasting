## Research applied

[Schefzik, Thorarinsdottir and Gneiting, ensemble copula coupling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.7149) explains why calibrated individual forecast marginals do not establish the dependence required for joint weather quantities. ECC reorders samples using raw ensemble ranks. NBH text supplies hourly location/spread rather than raw members, so this addition does **not** claim ECC or recovered meteorological dependence. It explicitly experiments with one common Student-t rank shock and computes bounds for arbitrary dependence of the declared marginal distributions. Empirical dependence templates remain unsupported by the small current history.

[ECMWF temporal-parameter documentation](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/CKB/pages/85402030/ERA5%2Bterminology%2Banalysis%2Band%2Bforecast%2Btime%2Band%2Bsteps%2Binstantaneous%2Band%2Baccumulated%2Band%2Bmean%2Brates%2Band%2Bmin%2Bmax%2Bparameters) distinguishes instantaneous temperatures from interval extrema. Accordingly, our daily distribution is labelled a piecewise-linear hourly **proxy**; its dependence bounds do not establish bounds on real subhour extrema or TWC/source discrepancies. These are research-informed engineering choices, not demonstrated accuracy gains.

## Calculation and consumers

For each hourly point, `F_i(x) = t_5((x - TMP_i) / scale_i)`, with `scale_i = max(1 F, TSD_i sqrt(3/5))`. Boundary location and scale interpolate linearly. No independence-based uncertainty reduction occurs. The common-rank maximum CDF is `min_i F_i`; minimum CDF is `max_i F_i`. By the union bound, an arbitrary-dependence maximum CDF lies between `max(0, 1 - sum_i(1-F_i))` and `min_i F_i`; a minimum CDF lies between `max_i F_i` and `min(1, sum_i F_i)`. These bounds are conditional on the experimental marginals.

Whole-degree venue brackets use the existing half-degree boundaries. For bracket `(a,b]`, lower probability is `max(0,L(b)-U(a))`, upper is `min(1,U(b)-L(a))`. Bounds may be broad; that information is retained. `probabilityBand` widens the actual candidate band with these bounds, refuses invalid bounds and still requires the existing empirical venue/date and probability-bin admission gates. The allocator uses the resulting lower probability. No new model is pre-admitted and no orders are placed.

The target is a 24-hour local **standard-time** climate day. Every remaining hour and the final exclusive boundary must have valid NBH temperature and spread. A missing hour, missing spread, stale/future source or uncovered day gives an explicit gap. A first forecast may be held back at most one hour, with that uncalibrated assumption recorded. Current-day calculations require at least three prior observations and a last reading within 90 minutes. A loose NWS floor/ceiling is a proxy witness; a declared, untrained 1% source-disagreement mixture keeps outcomes beyond that witness possible. Future-day calculations require complete future-day coverage and do not manufacture observation anchors.

`weather_weather_station_evidence` returns `dailyExtremes.forecasts` and `.gaps` without consulting Kalshi. `weather_weather_evidence` retrieves the same station projection by immutable SHA-256 evidence ID. The raw NOAA/NWS packet is archived separately before derived forecasts; derived availability is the local compilation time. The source packet and complete hourly path remain recoverable. `nbm-nbh-comonotone-high-v1` and `nbm-nbh-comonotone-low-v1` are additional daily market competitors, excluded from existing family pools to avoid double-counting NBM. Venue binary outcomes use the existing definitive-settlement scorer; independent daily physical grading is not yet implemented.
