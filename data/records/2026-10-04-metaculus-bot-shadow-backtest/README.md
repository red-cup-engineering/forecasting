# Shadow backtest, 2026-10-04: how it was run and what it can claim

Replay of the bot's recipe on resolved Polymarket binary markets (Metaculus's API needs a bot token, so Metaculus resolved questions are not yet available to us). `RESULT.md` is the scored table (regenerate: `node scripts/metaculus-backtest.mjs score --dir . --min-members 2`).

- Sample: 40 resolved two-outcome Yes/No markets, one per event, ending 2026-06-21 to 2026-09-20, volume >= $10k-20k, as-of = end minus 7 days, market price at as-of from CLOB `prices-history`, markets already at <3% or >97% at as-of dropped. YES rate 0.25. Heavy on the 2026 US-Iran conflict, World Cup, US primaries and oil/BTC thresholds. Not a Metaculus-like question mix.
- Evidence arm: Google News RSS with `after:/before:` the as-of date plus GDELT `enddatetime`. Best-effort point in time; headlines can still carry later-edited pages, and some headlines quote Polymarket odds, which leaks the baseline into the bot's inputs.
- Executor: the MetroPlat MCP tools were used by hand from an agent session, because the shell holds no MCP client credentials (and none were read). Batch prompts in `batches/` are the script's canonical output; the prompts actually sent for retrieval batches 01 and 02 were retyped from them with rule text and some headlines trimmed. Batch 00 and the no-retrieval batch were sent as generated, modulo retyping. Answers append to `answers.jsonl` via `exec-add.mjs`.
- Coverage is partial and says so: retrieval arm 30 of 40 questions, members Qwen3.8-27B (Groq) and Llama-3.2-11B (NVIDIA) only (the third family failed: Gemma HTTP 500/timeout, Cohere aborted a 10-item prompt, Antigravity busy); no-retrieval arm 20 of 40, Qwen only. The ensemble in the table is therefore a 2-family, 30-question ensemble, not the bot's production recipe. Running `run --arm retrieval|no-retrieval` on the node (with MCP credentials in env) completes it.
- Contamination: the models' training cutoffs are not known to us. Qwen's no-retrieval answers on 2026 events were priors, not memory (it gave 0.85-0.95 to Hormuz reopening and a diplomatic meeting that did not happen as framed), which argues against leakage, and the leak probe (accuracy 0.70 early vs 0.90 late half) shows no sign of remembered outcomes. Neither is proof.
- n is small. Intervals are paired bootstrap over questions, one market per event, but 30 is still 30.
