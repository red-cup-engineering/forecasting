# Earnings-call mention markets: does remembering past calls beat the market? (junior engineer slice, 2026-10-04)

Research and backtest only. Public unsigned Kalshi GETs (`createKalshiClient({credentials: null})`, ~200 ms apart, backoff on 429) and public SEC EDGAR; no orders, no keys, no .env. Rules and fees as in `../2026-10-04-kalshi-live-mentions/README.md` (quadratic taker fee 0.07*P*(1-P), ceil to the cent per order; here orders of 10).

## Verdict

NO LANE. History is real information, and the market already contains it. Out of sample the model is much worse than the market (Brier 0.225 vs 0.164) and adds almost nothing given the market price (stacked Brier gain +0.0011 per market, +0.002 log loss: statistically positive, economically nothing). Trading its disagreements loses the fee: EV per contract at T-1h is -3.2c [-5.4, -0.8] with history features, about 0.0c [-2.5, +2.7] with the EDGAR press-release feature added, event-clustered 95% CI, 192 test events. No `shadow` CLI was written, per the brief.

Falsifier (what would reopen it): a model whose stacked out-of-sample Brier gain over the market mid exceeds roughly 0.01 per market with a CI above zero on a fresh later time block, or an EV after fee whose event-clustered CI excludes zero at >= 100 contracts of ask depth, using this exact pipeline (`scripts/earnings-mentions-*.mjs` re-run over a later quarter; the cache is regenerated by the collector). Untested lever: features from the text of the company's previous calls (what was actually said, including words that were never listed as strikes) and the prior-quarter transcript sentiment; Kalshi's results only cover listed strikes, and EDGAR press releases proved to carry real signal (below) but not enough.

## Data (collector: `scripts/earnings-mentions-collect.mjs`)

- GET /series: 190 earnings series (`KXEARNINGSMENTION*` 177 plus the older-named `KXMENTIONEARN*`). Settled markets from `/markets?status=settled` plus `/historical/markets` (live cutoff 2026-08-05; the history is split between the two): 5,946 markets, 434 events.
- Call start comes from `/milestones` (category Mentions, `start_date`): 5,127 markets / 387 events / 172 companies have it (2025-09-09 to 2026-10-01). The rest are dropped. 5,125 have result yes/no and 1-minute candlesticks over [T-26h, T+10min] (live `/series/{s}/markets/{t}/candlesticks` or `/historical/markets/{t}/candlesticks`, whose fields drop the `_dollars` suffix).
- Prices: executable yes quote (bid/ask close of the last minute candle at or before T-24h, T-1h, T-5min; empty books 0/1 rejected). Mid = (bid+ask)/2. Buying NO costs 1 - yes bid. 5,059 markets have a T-1h quote, 4,124 have one at T-24h. Base rate YES 0.53 (fit half 0.57, test 0.51).
- Caches live in the session scratchpad, not the repo. Mention strikes are single words ("Tariff" is 244 of the 5,946), phrases, "A / B" alternatives and "(N+ times)" counts.
- EDGAR (`scripts/earnings-mentions-edgar.mjs`): per event the 8-K item 2.02 accepted before the call start (descriptive UA with contact), exhibit text cached; 340 of 387 events found (ticker = series suffix mapped by SEC company_tickers.json), 4,513 markets. Feature: word occurs in the release under Kalshi's plural/possessive/slash/count rules (`releaseMentions`).

## Model (`src/earnings-mentions.mjs`; tests `test/earnings-mentions.test.mjs`, 9 pass)

Features for (company, word, quarter) use only calls with strictly earlier start (ties do not see each other): shrunk company-word YES rate (prior = word rate across companies) and its history length; resolved yes/no/not-listed 1, 2 and 4 calls ago; shrunk company YES rate; shrunk word rate across companies; company history length; count / multi-word / slash strike flags; with EDGAR: release hit, release miss (release found, word absent), log count. Ridge logistic regression (ridge 1), time split by call start at the median scoreable event (split 2026-04-28): fit half = 191 events, test half = 192 events (2,708 markets). The fit half is split again: model A (the earlier half of the fit half) predicts fold B out of fold; the stacking weights and the trade threshold are chosen on fold B only; the final model on the whole fit half is applied to the untouched test half.

## Results (test half, T-1h quote; `backtest-*.json` hold everything)

| | n | Brier | log loss |
| base rate | 2708 | 0.2536 | 0.700 |
| market mid | 2708 | 0.1642 | 0.496 |
| history model | 2708 | 0.2250 | 0.641 |
| history + EDGAR model | 2708 | 0.2149 | 0.618 |
| stack(market, history model) | 2708 | 0.1631 | 0.493 |
| stack(market, history + EDGAR) | 2708 | 0.1634 | 0.493 |

Model minus market Brier: +0.061 worse [0.053, 0.068] (history), +0.051 worse (with EDGAR). Market-residual skill, Brier gain of stack over market: history +0.0011 [+0.0002, +0.0021]; with EDGAR +0.0008 [-0.0007, +0.0025]; same at T-24h with EDGAR: 0.0000 [-0.0037, +0.0036] (market Brier 0.179 at T-24h vs 0.164 at T-1h, so the market learns between T-24h and T-1h, and the model does not improve with it). The stack weight on the model is -0.12 (history) and +0.25 (EDGAR): the market quote dominates (weight ~1). The strongest history signal is the shrunk company-word rate (coefficient 0.76 in logit) and, with EDGAR, "release found and word absent" (-0.51); EDGAR cuts model Brier 0.225 to 0.215, still far from the market.

Economics (buy the side whose model probability exceeds its ask by more than the threshold; threshold 0.20 chosen on fold B from {0.05..0.25}; the history grid was negative at every threshold, -1.8c to -3.1c; EV per contract after fee, event-clustered bootstrap of 2,000 draws):

| entry | features | trades | events | hit | mean price | EV/ct after fee |
| T-24h | history | 761 | 172 | 0.209 | 0.223 | -2.6c [-5.5, +0.4] |
| T-1h | history | 1011 | 192 | 0.178 | 0.199 | -3.2c [-5.4, -0.8] |
| T-5min | history | 1040 | 192 | 0.171 | 0.194 | -3.4c [-5.5, -1.1] |
| T-24h | history+EDGAR | 752 | 175 | 0.259 | 0.246 | +0.1c [-3.1, +3.2] |
| T-1h | history+EDGAR | 992 | 191 | 0.224 | 0.213 | 0.0c [-2.5, +2.7] |
| T-5min | history+EDGAR | 1001 | 192 | 0.195 | 0.209 | -2.5c [-4.8, -0.1] |

Most trades are in the cheap tail (mean price ~0.2) where the favoured side is the longshot-priced NO or YES at 0.1-0.3, where the 1-3c fee-rounded cost is a big fraction. The best rows are coin flips around zero (the EDGAR threshold grid on fold B ran -0.2c to +0.9c; the model's edge over the ask is smaller than the fee plus its own noise).

Liquidity: not measured from a tape because there is no positive EV to size. Candlesticks carry volume but not ask depth; the other lane's finding (median ~15k contracts per market lifetime) suggests depth is not the binding constraint, the edge is.

## Model-free check on the market itself (`scripts/earnings-mentions-calibration.mjs`, `market-calibration-h1.json`)

Post hoc, so read it as a lead and not a result. At T-1h the market mid exceeds the YES frequency in almost every band (e.g. mid 0.60 vs freq 0.49 fit, 0.57 test; 0.78 vs 0.73/0.74), a 2-6 point optimism, but buying NO at the ask earns about 0 after fee in both halves (+0.0001 to +0.014) except two isolated cells that flipped sign between halves (0.50-0.70: +6.7c fit, 0.0c test; 0.85-0.99: -3.1c fit, +0.7c test; 0.02-0.15: +0.3c fit, +3.3c test). One of 12 cells clearing a CI on a single half is what noise gives. The optimism is smaller than the fee-and-spread round trip.

## Caveats

- Quotes come from candlestick closes (a minute's last bid/ask), not a tape; fills at the ask are assumed. Spread is the 1-2c of the book.
- Call start is the milestone's scheduled start, not the actual first word; T-5min is close to it, not exact. Events whose milestone is missing (about 14% of markets) were dropped, which may select well-covered companies.
- Ties and shared quarters: companies report in clusters (a few days), so the earlier half contains most of the history the later half could see; a longer record than 13 months is the thing that would help the history features most.
- EDGAR text matching is a regex over the exhibit text (first exhibit named 99.x); tables and spacing quirks can miss, so release hits are a lower bound.
