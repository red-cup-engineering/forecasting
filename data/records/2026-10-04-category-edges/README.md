# Category deep-dive: politics, commodities, culture, science and tech, 2026-10-04

Research and paper only. Public Kalshi data (no credentials, no orders), plus the few external sources named below. New files only; `data/` is git-ignored (`data/.gitignore`), about 320 MB, regenerable. Scripts in `scripts/` (a copy lives in the Junior engineer's scratch dir), tables in `results/`.

Reproduce (from `scripts/`): `python3 fetch.py <window_hours> SERIES...` (env `MINVOL`, `MINCLOSE`), `python3 politics.py && python3 politics_eval.py`, `python3 fetch_min.py KXTRUMPAPPROVE 120 && python3 politics_min.py`, `python3 commod.py SERIES... && python3 commod_eval.py SERIES... && python3 commod_dig.py KXWTI 0.03`, `python3 winner_cal.py SERIES...`, `python3 spacex.py`.

Method common to all four: one hourly (or 1-minute) Kalshi candle snapshot per market at fixed horizons before close, quotes forward-filled from the last candle, taker fills at the candle-close ask (or at 1-bid for NO) with Kalshi's quadratic fee rounded UP per single contract (conservative; a 10-lot rounds to less), confidence intervals by bootstrap over EVENTS (not markets or snapshots), and the first half of events by close date as "fit" and the second half as "test". Candle-close quotes carry no depth; the live KXWTI books I read have 5 to 100 contracts at the touch. Maker EV is not simulated: the microstructure record's result (fill odds dominated by arrival rate, not queue; 10-25% per few hours for quiet markets) applies and would scale any maker EV down by that factor.

## 1. Politics and elections

Settlement sources (series metadata): KXTRUMPAPPROVE settles on the "Approve" column of the RealClearPolling "RCP Average" row at exactly 1:00 PM ET; KXVHGCB on the VoteHub 2026 generic ballot margin (D minus R, time-weighted) at 5:05 PM ET. Both events open at 14:00Z the day before and close a minute before the reading (the market stays open until 12:59 ET, so there is no lag between the last tradable minute and the reading).

Access: `realclearpolling.com/robots.txt` returns a DataDome captcha page (not readable by automation, and I did not try to defeat it); `votehub.com/robots.txt` carries a notice that scraping and automated collection of its data are prohibited without written permission. So the "average is a deterministic function of published polls" idea cannot be measured by reading the polls or the averages here. What can be measured without them: the settled values themselves (the `expiration_value` field is the RCP/VoteHub series), and Kalshi's pricing path into the reading.

Data: 32 approve events (2026-09-02 to 10-03; 288 markets: 7 exact 0.1-point bins plus a below and an above tail) and 9 VoteHub events (79 markets). Tiny: every CI below is wide.

* Daily change of the settled value (`results/politics_delta.json`): approve mean absolute change 0.17 point, 32% of days exactly unchanged, sd 0.26, 5th/95th percentile -0.55/+0.45; GCB mean absolute change 0.19, sd 0.26.
* **Where the information arrives** (mean mid on the winning exact bin, approve; `results/politics_eval.json`, `politics_minute.json`): 0.28 at 24h before close, 0.35 at 6h, 0.43 at 3h, 0.70 at 1h, then by minute 0.80 at T-60, 0.85 at T-30, 0.89 at T-15, 0.92 at T-5, 0.96 at T-1. GCB: 0.36, 0.55, 0.59, 0.90 at the same hourly marks. The mass moves in the last two hours, i.e. when the day's polls are added. The market's favourite bin is the winner 90% of the time at T-60, 94% at T-15 and 100% at T-5 (approve).
* **Market vs a persistence model** (yesterday's value plus the fit-half change distribution, half empirical and half normal; `politics_eval.json`): log loss over bins, test half, approve: market 0.257/0.210/0.217/0.184/0.053 at 24/12/6/3/1h before close, model 0.30 throughout. At 24h the model beats the market in the fit half (0.31 vs 0.37) and loses in the test half (0.30 vs 0.26): not stable.
* **EV, taker, model-signalled** (|model - ask| above 3c after fee, event-clustered CI): approve fit half 24h +15.7c [+1.8, +30.7] (38 trades, 14 events); test half -4.3c [-12.7, +4.3] (67 trades, 16 events). At every other horizon, both halves, the point estimate is negative (test: -2.8, -1.3, -2.4, -3.7c, the last CI [-5.7, -1.1]). GCB: no horizon positive in the fit half; test half CIs straddle 0 (n = 5 events). **No static edge.**
* **EV of reading RCP live (oracle upper bound)**, approve: buy the bin that ends up winning at the ask T minutes before close, certain of the final value: T-90 +13.4c [+5.9, +22.9], T-60 +10.8c [+4.7, +18.6], T-30 +5.7c [+2.3, +11.0], T-15 +3.8c [+1.2, +7.9], T-5 +1.4c [+0.2, +3.3] (31-32 events; after fee). GCB (9 events): +14.9, +12.3, +7.9, +6.6, +9.0c at T-90..T-5 with CIs reaching 0. These are bounds: they assume the RCP number visible at T-minus stays the settled number (a later poll can move it; the market's own T-15 favourite is wrong 6% of the time), and the depth is unknown (open interest per bin is 1,000 to 11,000 contracts, so size is not the constraint, the information is).
* Verdict: the lag is real but short (the market catches up between 10:00 and 12:30 ET) and anyone reading RCP's page at 12:30 to 12:55 ET can still earn 2 to 6c per contract. Doing that is human-assisted (the assisted browser) rather than an automated lane because the source blocks automation. Worth a logged paper experiment: snapshot the RCP value by hand at 12:30, 12:45, 12:55 ET for 20 days and score it against the book. An automated lane would need a licensed or permitted poll feed (RCP and VoteHub both decline), so the polls-to-average computation stays unmeasured.
* Not done: election-outcome markets against polling models (538 discontinued; Silver Bulletin not read; no settled 2026 general-election markets in the window, the midterms are 2026-11-03).

## 2. Commodities

Settlement sources: KXGOLDD/KXSILVERD/KXBRENTD/KXNATGASD: Pyth 1-minute candle close at 5:00 PM EDT (gold, silver and Brent and natgas Pyth indices); KXWTI and KXWTIW: ICE daily settlement of the nearest WTI contract (14:30 ET), rolling two business days before expiry. Reference used: Yahoo Finance chart JSON for the front-month futures (GC=F, SI=F, BZ=F, NG=F, CL=F), 5-minute bars for the last 60 days (5 requests; an unofficial endpoint, research-only, flag for a licensed feed before anything automated). Not read: CME public pages (no free historical settlement file found, not attempted), EIA (API key needed; robots allows the site; the Wednesday 10:30 ET petroleum release event study was NOT done), Pyth history (Hermes archive returned 401; benchmarks shim 404 for the symbols tried), Stooq (proof-of-work gate, not circumvented).

Model: S_T ~ Normal(F_t + b, sigma^2 tau + sigma_b^2), F_t the futures last price at the snapshot, b the previous settled event's (Kalshi settle value minus futures price at that close) so no look-ahead, sigma the root mean square 5-minute return over the trailing 5 days scaled by the expected count of trading bars to the settle (schedule taken from 7 days earlier), sigma_b the expanding std of daily basis changes. A vol multiplier k in {0.8 .. 2.0} is chosen on the fit half by log loss. Quotes: spread at most 10c, mid 3 to 97c. 31 to 39 events per series (Aug 6 to Oct 1, markets with at least 1,000 contracts traded), 718 to 4,469 priced market-snapshots per series (`results/commod_eval.json`).

* Calibration of the model itself (z = (settle - forecast)/model sd): sd about 0.93 to 0.97 at 6 to 24h for WTI, 0.86 to 1.35 for gold, 0.76 to 0.86 for Brent, 0.98 to 1.14 for natgas: roughly calibrated. WTI mean z is +0.2 (a +0.3 dollar average miss upward in this window; drift is part of the YES-side gain below).
* **Gold, silver, natgas:** market log loss is as low or lower than the model at most horizons; EV after fee in the test half has CIs straddling zero at every horizon (gold 24h +7.9c [-7.0, +24.0]; silver 24h +14c [-2, +33], 12h +10c [-6, +26] with fit-half +19c, +9c; natgas 24h +13c [-50, +60]). Silver 24h is positive in both halves but with CIs including 0 and 5 horizons x 5 series looked at; treat as noise.
* **Brent:** the model is bad (the Pyth Brent index and BZ=F disagree around rolls); the test-half EV at 1 to 3h is **negative** (-8.8c, CI [-17, -2]): the model is wrong, the market right. Not usable without the Pyth Brent index itself.
* **KXWTI (ICE settle) is the lead.** 34 events, 740 signalled trades pooled over 12/6/3/1h: +9.0c per contract [+3.9, +14.8]; fit half +8.5c [-1.8, +20.5], test half +9.3c [+3.9, +15.7]; 12h+6h only: +12.1c [+4.4, +19.5]; robust to the signal threshold (0c: +6.3c [+2.7, +10.3] on 1,311 trades; 6c: +10.5c; 10c: +11.3c); YES side +10.9c [+2.7, +19.7], NO side +6.5c [-2.9, +15.9]; hour volume at least 500 subset +9.7c [+0.1, +18.8]. Caveats that keep this a lead, not an edge: (a) 34 events, one regime (a rising window, mean z +0.2); (b) one series of six, five horizons and two thresholds were examined, so a Bonferroni-style correction over six series puts the pooled p near 0.05; (c) quotes are hourly-close touch quotes with 5 to 100 contracts of depth in the live book (the daily ladders show 0.76/0.99 style spreads), so the capacity is on the order of tens of contracts per strike and about a dollar to tens of dollars a day; (d) fee modelled as 1-lot rounded up; (e) the Yahoo CL=F roll may differ from the ICE roll rule near expiry. The mechanism is plausible (a thin, wide, slow ladder against a liquid underlying) but unproven.
* KXWTIW (weekly ladder, 7 events) and the 15-minute series are too short here to say anything; the 15-minute Pyth markets (gold/WTI/silver 15M, hundreds of millions of contracts) are the efficient end and were not studied.
* Verdict: a zero-risk shadow for KXWTI daily only: log the model probability from a licensed or permitted futures feed against the live ladder each hour until about 60 events are in; promote only if the held-out lower bound stays positive after the 5-100 contract depth is respected. No lane for gold, silver, natgas, Brent.

## 3. Culture and entertainment

Settlement sources: Netflix Top 10 (US shows, movies; weekly, published Tuesday, market closes Monday 11:59 PM ET so the week is fully over and unpublished at close), Billboard Hot 100 and 200 (chart dated Saturday, published the Tuesday before the date, market closes the Monday of that week 11:59 PM ET, after the tracking week ended Thursday and before publication), Spotify Charts daily (no settled markets in the listing), Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer at 10:00 AM ET (KXRT).

Reference access: **Rotten Tomatoes skipped** (the site's terms restrict automated reads; I did not read it). Box Office Mojo/IMDb conditions prohibit automated access, The Numbers only blocks some bots but no box-office series appeared in the Kalshi Entertainment category list anyway. Billboard robots.txt names AI agents as disallowed. Spotify Charts requires login. FlixPatrol disallows ClaudeBot. kworb.net allows crawlers but only publishes the latest day, no history to score against. So no external reference was read; this section is Kalshi-only calibration of the winner market against its own outcome, and the potential edge (the data exist, unpublished, at market close) is described, not measured.

Method (`winner_cal.py`): favourite = top-mid market in each event at 72/24/6/1h before close; EV of buying the favourite at its ask and of fading it (buying NO at 1 - bid), after fee; event-clustered CIs; fit/test split.

| series | events | fav price / hit rate at 24h | fav EV at ask, 24h | fade EV, 24h | test half |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Netflix US shows | 89 | 0.871 / 0.851 | -3.3c [-9.9, +2.4] | -5.4c [-11.4, +1.0] | -0.3c; fade -4.2c |
| Netflix US movies | 90 | 0.873 / 0.811 | -7.5c [-16.2, +0.2] | -0.2c [-7.4, +8.1] | -3.1c; fade -2.5c |
| Billboard Hot 100 #1 | 170 | 0.961 / 0.955 | -1.7c [-7.4, +2.8] | -5.7c [-10.2, -0.3] | +2.3c [+1.0, +4.0] (fit half -1.7c: no) |
| Billboard 200 #1 | 92 | 0.882 / 0.810 | -8.5c [-20.1, +1.6] | -4.5c [-19.0, +9.8] | -10.7c |
| LM Arena top model (daily) | 53 | 0.898 / 0.886 | -2.2c [-9.6, +3.2] | -2.2c [-8.1, +5.3] | -2.2c |

Every cell loses money buying at the ask or fading at the bid except the Hot 100 test half, which does not repeat in the fit half. The favourite is slightly overpriced for movies and the Billboard 200 at 72 to 24h (hit 75 to 81% at 84 to 88c) but the fade pays the spread and its EV is not positive. At Netflix close (h1) the favourite still trades at 0.87 with an 83% hit rate in the 47 of 89 weeks that were not already locked at 99c, even though the week is over; that is the unexploited public-information gap, and it is closeable only by a source that reports the daily or weekly rank before Tuesday's publication, which I could not read within terms.
* Verdict: no shadow lane. If Netflix's own weekly file or a licensed Spotify/Luminate feed becomes available, the right first test is predicting the Monday-night favourite against the 0.87 price.

## 4. Science and tech

Settlement sources (series metadata): SpaceX launches: SpaceX/FAA counting rules (KXSPACEXCOUNT monthly count); top AI model: LM Arena leaderboard (without style control) at 10:00 AM ET (KXTOPMODEL); release dates: company announcement (KXCLAUDE, KXGPT, KXIPHONERELEASE); FDA: the FDA's own approval (KXFDAAPPROVE).
* **SpaceX monthly count** (`scripts/spacex.py`; Launch Library 2 free API, 3 calls): the month is a count, so after the (N+1)th successful launch "more than N" is decided. In the three cases where LL2's count matches Kalshi's (KXSPACEXCOUNT-26SEP-7, 26AUG-11, 26AUG-10) the decided strike was already at 0.98 to 0.99 within 0.2 to 0.6 hours of the launch. LL2 lists 14 August launches and Kalshi settled the month at 13, so in the other two (26AUG-12 and 26AUG-13) LL2's count said "decided" a launch too early: Aug-12 stayed at 0.87 to 0.89 and settled YES, Aug-13 priced 0.00 and settled NO. The market applies Kalshi's counting rule better than the public tracker does; there is no lag edge in completed launches. The residual question (future launches) cannot be back-tested because LL2 does not keep schedule snapshots; live today: Oct count already 3 (LL2), three "Go" launches through Oct 13, the rest of the manifest TBD, and the market prices more-than-10 at 0.42 to 0.44 and more-than-9 at 0.84, consistent with the 13/14 and 8 of the last three months. No signal.
* **Top AI model** (53 daily events): efficient (table above); LM Arena is behind Cloudflare and was not read.
* **Release-date and FDA ladders:** only 3 events each for KXCLAUDE/KXGPT/KXIPHONERELEASE/KXFDAAPPROVE settled in the listing. Too few to calibrate; FDA decision dates (PDUFA) are announced by sponsors, not by an FDA feed, and Apple's event dates are announced days ahead of the release date, so a public schedule does exist for those two but an event-clustered test needs a collector that snapshots both the schedule and the book.
* Verdict: no shadow lane now. A cheap forward collector (LL2 upcoming snapshots, Apple/FDA announcement times, book snapshots) is the only way to learn whether schedules predict outcomes before repricing.

## Honest negatives and unknowns

Politics: the poll-to-average computation is unmeasured (sources blocked); only the oracle bound is measured. Commodities: a single positive lead among six series, confined to the daily WTI ladder, on 34 events. Culture: no external data read at all (terms), so it is calibration only. Science: sample too small outside SpaceX counts and top-model. No order was placed; no code outside this directory was touched.

## Addendum 2026-10-04: KXWTI paper shadow (family `wti`) and its admission bar

The section 2 lead is now a log-only fifth family in the category-shadows runner (`src/category-shadows/wti.mjs`, `node scripts/category-shadows.mjs tick --only wti`). Paper only: public Kalshi reads and the Yahoo CL=F chart JSON (the backtest's source; one 60-day 5-minute request, cached 4 minutes, made only while a window is open), no orders, no credentials.

Model and rule are the backtest's, unchanged (`commod.py`, `commod_dig.py`): Normal(F + b, sig^2 + sb^2) with F the last completed 5-minute CL=F close, b the previous settled event's ICE settle minus CL=F at that event's close, sig from the trailing 5-day rms 5-minute return scaled by the bar count of the same window 7 days earlier, sb the population std of earlier daily basis changes (at least 5), vol multiplier 1.0; taker at the ask (YES) or 1 - bid (NO) when the edge after the 1-lot fee exceeds 3c and ask > bid. Horizons 12, 6, 3 and 1 hours before the 14:30 ET close (the pooled set behind the +9.0c figure); every bracket of the daily event is logged once per event and horizon (bid/ask, sizes, refP, edge, fee10, would-take flag), snapshot taken when the time to close is within 20 minutes after the horizon mark. Daily events only (`KXWTI-YYMMMDD14`).

Deviations from the backtest, all forced by live operation: (1) the snapshot is taken at the first tick inside the 20-minute window, using the CL=F price at that moment rather than at the exact hour mark; (2) quotes are the live touch book with sizes, not hourly candle closes, and the logged fee10 is the 10-lot fee while the signal threshold uses the backtest's 1-lot fee; (3) the settled-event history for b and sb is the last 58 days (Yahoo's 5-minute limit), not Aug 6 onward; (4) the 24h horizon the eval also measured is not logged.

Admission bar (fixed in advance, `BARS.wti` in `grade.mjs`; change only by a dated amendment): at least 60 settled events with a would-take row, on at least 20 UTC days, mean EV at least +3c per contract after fee, and a positive lower bound of the 95% cluster bootstrap clustered by event (the horizon rows of one event are one cluster). Retire if the mean is at most 0 after 25 events. Passing the bar admits nothing to capital by itself; the 5-100 contract touch depth must be respected in any sizing.
